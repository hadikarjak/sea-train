OUR JOURNEY

From Local Courier to Global Logistics Leader

Sea Train Lines was founded in 2005 by a team of logistics professionals who saw an opportunity to revolutionize the shipping industry. What started as a small courier service with just three vans in Dubai has grown into an international freight forwarding company with offices in 15 countries and partnerships across 120+ nations.

Our growth has been driven by a simple philosophy: put the customer first and leverage technology to provide transparency. We were among the first in the region to offer real-time tracking, and we continue to invest in cutting-edge solutions that give our clients complete visibility over their supply chains.

Today, we manage over 15,000 shipments monthly, serving everything from small e-commerce businesses to multinational corporations. But despite our growth, we've maintained the personalized approach that set us apart from day one.
